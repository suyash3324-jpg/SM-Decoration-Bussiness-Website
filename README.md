🎊 SM Mandap Decoration
A modern, responsive website for SM Mandap Decoration, a decoration business specializing in weddings, birthday parties, and other events. Built as a freelance project to help the business showcase its work and connect with customers online.
✨ Features
🏠 Attractive home page with hero banner and business introduction
🖼️ Gallery of past decoration work (weddings, birthdays, receptions, etc.)
💍 Services page: mandap, stage, floral, balloon, and theme decorations
📞 Contact section with phone, WhatsApp, and enquiry form
📍 Business location and working details
📱 Fully responsive: works on mobile, tablet, and desktop
⚡ Fast loading with Vite and optimized images
